# Code of Conduct

ROSETTA is built by volunteers with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone is welcome here.

## We expect

- Respect and patience, above all with people who are new to GitHub or to the subject.
- Comments about the work, not the person.
- Credit given where it is due: say whose idea or finding you are building on.
- Honest reporting: say what you tested and what you did not.
- Sinhala, Tamil and English speakers treated equally; no language is "the main one".

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Sharing anyone's personal information without their permission.
- Claiming others' work as your own.
- Political or religious arguments unrelated to the work.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers by email to the repository owner (address on the owner's GitHub profile), or through a private message to a maintainer. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or remove a contributor who breaks this code.
